# Feature-linked controller workflows

These 25 requirements extend the persistent complete implementation goal. They
supplement existing ledgers; shared engines must satisfy overlapping workflows
without duplicating state. Each complete workflow remains OPEN pending its
required source, native interaction, supported backend and physical evidence.
Recommendations and a visible control do not prove executable machine behavior.

| # | Requirement | Completion evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Shared contextual selection | Scene and workbench retain one selection across relevant editors and tabs. |
| 2 | Illustrated geometry editors | Editing a dimension highlights its corresponding drawing and rendered geometry. |
| 3 | Setup revision review | Changes expose exact dependencies, reversible drafts and invalidated checks. |
| 4 | Intent command palette | Contextual commands explain missing prerequisites and open their remedies. |
| 5 | Machining-context program picker | Thumbnails, stock, tooling, WCS and compatibility are visible before selection. |
| 6 | Program revision comparison | Changed motion, tooling, depths, feeds, timing and clearance consequences are compared. |
| 7 | Focused running workspace | Actual operation, progress, interventions and camera dominate during execution. |
| 8 | Synchronized event timeline | Transport, telemetry, camera, commands and source context retain timing and gaps. |
| 9 | Actionable diagnostics | Errors distinguish transport, stale data, camera and machine causes with resolving checks. |
| 10 | Operator ergonomics profiles | Units, density, panes, shortcuts and pendant preferences persist separately from machine configuration. |
| 11 | Feature-based probing | Selected geometry binds previewed approach and qualified measured datum transactions. |
| 12 | Feature inspection plans | Nominals, tolerances, repetitions and measurements produce linked inspection reports. |
| 13 | Measure-correct-finish | Bounded radial/diametral corrections are previewed, applied through capable backends and reinspected. |
| 14 | Surface cleanup planning | Measured high spots, unknown regions, cleanup depth and remaining thickness are explicit. |
| 15 | Complete physical tool assemblies | CAD, holders, collets, stickout, pockets and calibration bind reach and clearance. |
| 16 | Tool life and sister management | Observed cutting exposure and wear inform compatible replacements and required revalidation. |
| 17 | Two-bank execution | Grouping, safe reload, physical reconciliation, calibration and continuation are exercised. |
| 18 | Residual-stock engagement prediction | Remaining material and cutter geometry expose entry, corner and engagement transitions. |
| 19 | Operation-specific adaptation | Process-specific bounded interventions combine available effort, RPM, feed, engagement and freshness. |
| 20 | Qualified local fast supervision | Measured latency/planner effects and local protections govern actual supported adaptive execution. |
| 21 | Rotary setup assistant | Workholding, centerline, zero, direction, timing, limits and unwinding are qualified. |
| 22 | Five-axis configuration review | Alternative solutions, singularities, transitions, clearance and TCP ownership are visible and backend-qualified. |
| 23 | Pallet and fixture instances | Part instances bind WCS, probing and scheduling with sequential versus tool-grouped comparison. |
| 24 | Supervised peripherals | Supported I/O has observed state, acknowledgements, coordinated sequences and recovery. |
| 25 | Qualified restart and recovery | Modal state, tooling, offsets, position confidence, stock and safe re-entry are reviewed and qualified. |
